About us

Good floors start with the prep

Rodney Timber Floor Sanding is Nick Coombe's business, based at Red Beach. Nick and his team sand and refinish timber floors in homes across the Hibiscus Coast, Rodney, the North Shore and the wider Auckland region, from original rimu and matai to American oak, kwila and engineered boards.

FAQ

Before you call

Anything else, ring Nick on 021 645 259.

What areas do you cover?

We're based at Red Beach and work across the Hibiscus Coast, Rodney, the North Shore and the wider Auckland region. Recent jobs include Snells Beach, Dairy Flat, Murrays Bay and Remuera.

How do I get a quote?

Call or email with your suburb, roughly how much floor there is, the timber if you know it, and a couple of photos. Nick will come back to you to talk through options and arrange a look at the floor.

Can you change the colour of my floor?

Yes. Recent jobs include taking the red tones out of kwila for a warm brown and lightening yellow matai to a sandy, natural look. Samples go down on your boards first so you can choose before the whole floor is done.

Water-based or solvent-based polyurethane?

Both are available, along with hard wax oils, in finishes from raw-look matte to gloss. Which suits depends on your timber, the look you want and how the room gets used, so ask Nick to walk you through it when he quotes.

Do you do decks as well?

Yes, exterior timber decks too. One recent client had a weathered kwila deck sanded back and oiled. Mention the deck when you get in touch.

What happens on the day?

Ask Nick when he quotes how long your floor will take and how long to stay off it between coats, as that depends on the finish. He'll let you know what needs moving and keep you updated through the job.
