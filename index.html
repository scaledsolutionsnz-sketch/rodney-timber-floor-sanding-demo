What we do for your floors

Sand & Coat

Rodney Timber Floor Sanding is run by Nick from Red Beach on the Hibiscus Coast. The work covers older homes with original floorboards through to newer houses with solid or engineered timber, across Rodney, the North Shore and the wider Auckland region.

Every job gets careful prep, attention to detail and clear communication from the first visit to the final coat, with a finish chosen to suit your household, not just the timber.

  • Owner-operated, so the person who quotes is the person who sands
  • Free quotes with straight answers on coating options
  • Tidy workmanship and a durable, high-quality finish
