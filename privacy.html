Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Rodney Timber Floor Sanding handles personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. This is a placeholder policy for this demo site and will be replaced with the business's own policy before launch.

What we collect

When you get in touch we may collect your name, phone number, email address, property address and details and photos of your floor. We only collect what you choose to give us.

How we use it

We use it to quote, schedule and carry out work, invoice for it, and reply to your enquiries. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Photos of finished floors

We sometimes photograph finished floors for our Facebook page. Photos do not show personal details, and we are happy to leave your job out if you ask.

This website

This site has no forms and does not set its own tracking cookies. The embedded Google Map and Facebook videos are provided by Google and Meta, and their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by calling 021 645 259.
