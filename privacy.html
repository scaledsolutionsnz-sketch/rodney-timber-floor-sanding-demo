Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Rodney Timber Floor Sanding respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. This page is placeholder content for the demo site and will be replaced with the business's final policy.

What we collect

When you contact us for a quote we may collect your name, phone number, email address, property address and details about your flooring. We only collect information you choose to give us.

How we use it

Your information is used to prepare quotes, schedule and carry out work, invoice for completed jobs, and respond to enquiries. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Photos of completed work

We sometimes photograph finished floors for our portfolio and social media. Photos never include personal identifying details, and we are happy to leave your job out on request.

Storage and security

Contact details are stored securely and kept only as long as needed for quoting, warranty and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by getting in touch on 021 645 259.
